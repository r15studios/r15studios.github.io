The starting point

More than a list of links

Raúl needed a place of his own where recruiters could understand his work quickly, then explore each project without being sent off to a loose collection of external links.

The challenge was to bring software, computer vision, generative AI and solution architecture work together without turning the site into a flat catalogue or an endless technical write-up.

The solution

A clear homepage. Case studies with substance.

The homepage opens with a direct professional proposition and leads into a project selection ordered by recency. Each card opens an internal case study with its own narrative, real images and a link to the original work when one is available.

Editorial hierarchy

The profile comes first, followed by the projects and then the technical detail.

Verifiable content

Real screenshots, results and limitations. Visual concepts are labelled as concepts, not presented as a deployed product.

International by design

Six complete versions with their own routes, language selector and tailored metadata.
