Case study · Real client · In preparation

A beauty website that explains before it sells

We are preparing Elena's website. She is a beauty and nail professional who needs to bring services, prices, real photographs and access to appointments together in one easy-to-share link.

The starting point

The information was scattered

Elena runs a one-person business. Her clients need to understand which treatments she offers, what they cost and how to book. Her Google profile is barely used and her current business name is not always easy to find.

She already pays for booking software that works well for her. The project will not replace it or add an unnecessary bot. The website will organise the information and lead clients to the booking flow she already uses.

The plan

Clear services. Real photographs. Straightforward booking.

The first version will be built when the final content arrives: each service with its explanation, price and photographs. Elena will review the complete website, tone and information before publication.

Easy-to-check services and prices

Treatments such as facial cleansing or diathermy will have a short explanation, price and clear next step.

Real work, not generated photographs

The images will come from Elena and her services. AI-generated images will not be presented as work produced by the studio.

Connected booking

The website will link to her current booking software rather than make her replace a tool she already likes.

Website in Spanish and English

The project will follow the R15 standard: mobile and desktop, ES/EN selector, social preview, custom 404 and accounts owned by the client.

Current status

Commission agreed. Content pending.

The €150 commission is agreed. Elena will send the services, prices and photographs needed for the first version in October.

Until then, this case study states only what is confirmed. It does not show fake screenshots or present the website as live.

What will be delivered

A simple website Elena can share

A fast website of her own

Services, prices, photographs and contact details in a mobile-first site.

Clear ownership and maintenance

The website will live in the client's account. Future changes can be handled by R15, with a €60-per-season maintenance option.

Review before publication

Nothing will go live until Elena has seen and approved the first version.
